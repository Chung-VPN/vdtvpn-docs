Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-17

VDTVPN is a client application used to import, manage, and use network connection configurations provided by the user or by third-party providers. The application does not provide built-in servers and does not include preloaded connection profiles in the APK.

1. Information the app may process

Depending on how the app is used, VDTVPN may process:

  • Subscription or configuration URLs entered by the user or scanned from a QR code.
  • Connection profiles downloaded from a user-provided subscription URL.
  • Device identifier/HWID used for account security, device limit protection, and abuse prevention.
  • Basic technical status such as selected profile, connection status, latency/ping, and local app settings.
  • Account-related information returned by a subscription provider, such as User ID, expiration date, or plan metadata.

VDTVPN does not collect contacts, SMS messages, photos, videos, personal files, call history, or GPS location.

2. How information is used

  • To import and manage connection profiles.
  • To fetch configurations from a subscription URL provided by the user.
  • To display connection status, latency, expiration information, and related technical details.
  • To support account security, device limit enforcement, and abuse prevention.
  • To provide support when the user voluntarily contacts support.

3. Android VPNService

VDTVPN uses Android VPNService to create a VPN connection when the user starts it. Network traffic may be routed according to the selected configuration. The app does not provide VPN servers. Users are responsible for the configuration sources and third-party services they choose to use.

4. Camera and QR codes

The camera permission is used only when the user chooses to scan a QR code. The camera is used to read the QR code content. VDTVPN does not record video, store camera images, or upload camera images.

5. Sharing of information

VDTVPN does not sell personal data. Data may be sent to a subscription URL or server configured by the user to fetch profiles or validate access. Those services may be operated by the user or by a third party, and their own policies may apply.

6. Local storage

Some data may be stored locally on the device, such as subscription URLs, downloaded profiles, selected server/profile, app settings, and device identifier/HWID. Users can remove local data by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.
