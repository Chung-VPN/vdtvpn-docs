Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-17

VDTVPN is a network client application that enables users to import, manage, and use their own connection profiles. The application does not include or distribute VPN servers, proxy services, or user accounts.

Information the app may process

  • Subscription or configuration URLs entered by the user or scanned from a QR code.
  • Connection profiles downloaded from a user-provided subscription URL.
  • Device identifier/HWID used for account security, device limit protection, and abuse prevention when required by a subscription provider.
  • Basic technical status such as selected profile, connection status, latency/ping, and local app settings.
  • Account-related information returned by a subscription provider, such as User ID, expiration date, or plan metadata.

VDTVPN does not collect contacts, SMS messages, photos, videos, personal files, call history, or GPS location.

How information is used

  • To import and manage connection profiles.
  • To fetch configurations from a subscription URL provided by the user.
  • To display connection status, latency, expiration information, and related technical details.
  • To support account security, device limits, and abuse prevention.
  • To provide support when the user voluntarily contacts support.

Android VPNService

VDTVPN uses Android VPNService to create a VPN connection only when the user starts it. Network traffic may be routed according to the selected configuration. VDTVPN does not provide VPN servers. Users are responsible for the configuration sources and third-party services they choose to use.

Camera and QR codes

The camera permission is used only when the user chooses to scan a QR code. The app reads QR code content and does not record video, store camera images, or upload camera images.

Sharing of information

VDTVPN does not sell personal data. Data may be sent to a subscription URL or server configured by the user to fetch profiles or validate access. Those services may be operated by the user or by a third party, and their own policies may apply.

Local storage

Some data may be stored locally on the device, such as subscription URLs, downloaded profiles, selected profile, app settings, and device identifier/HWID. Users can remove local data by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

Children

VDTVPN is not directed to children under 13. The app is intended for users who can manage their own network configuration profiles.
