Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-18

VDTVPN is an Android network client application that helps users import, manage, and use their own network connection profiles. VDTVPN uses Android VPNService only to create a VPN connection when the user starts a connection.

VDTVPN does not provide, sell, rent, or distribute VPN servers, proxy services, user accounts, or preloaded connection profiles.

Information We Process

VDTVPN may process the following information:

1. Connection profiles and subscription data

  • Subscription URLs entered by the user.
  • QR code content scanned by the user.
  • Configuration data imported from Clipboard.
  • Connection profiles downloaded from a user-provided subscription URL.
  • Selected server/profile, connection status, latency/ping, and app settings.

2. Device and technical information

VDTVPN may generate or use a device identifier/HWID for app functionality, account security, device-limit protection, abuse prevention, and compatibility with subscription providers selected by the user.

This may include or be derived from:

  • Android ID or app-scoped device identifier.
  • Device manufacturer, model, product name, Android version, SDK version, and package name.
  • App version and basic technical connection metadata.

3. Camera access

VDTVPN requests camera permission only when the user chooses to scan a QR code. The app uses the camera to read QR code content. VDTVPN does not record video, store camera images, or upload camera images.

4. Notification and foreground service

VDTVPN may use notifications and a foreground service to show VPN connection status while the VPN connection is active.

VDTVPN does not collect contacts, SMS messages, call logs, photos, videos, precise GPS location, microphone recordings, payment card information, or government ID information.

How We Use Information

VDTVPN uses information only for app functionality, including:

  • Importing and managing user-provided connection profiles.
  • Fetching configuration data from subscription URLs provided by the user.
  • Starting, maintaining, and stopping VPN connections using Android VPNService.
  • Showing connection status, speed, latency/ping, and selected profile information.
  • Supporting QR code import, Clipboard import, and subscription URL import.
  • Supporting device-limit protection, account security, fraud prevention, and abuse prevention when required by a user-selected subscription provider.
  • Providing support when the user voluntarily contacts us.

Android VPNService

VDTVPN uses Android VPNService to create a secure local VPN tunnel on the device when the user starts a connection. Network traffic may be routed through the server or connection profile selected by the user.

VDTVPN does not monitor, sell, or use VPN traffic for advertising. VDTVPN does not inject ads into network traffic and does not modify user traffic for unrelated purposes.

Users are responsible for the legality, safety, and reliability of the third-party configuration sources, subscription URLs, and servers they choose to use.

Sharing of Information

VDTVPN does not sell personal data.

Information may be sent to a subscription URL, server, or service configured by the user in order to download profiles, validate access, apply device limits, or establish a connection. These services may be operated by the user or by third-party providers, and their own privacy policies may apply.

VDTVPN may also share information if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the app, or others.

Data Security

VDTVPN uses reasonable technical measures to protect user data. When data is transmitted to subscription providers or configured servers, it should be transmitted using secure protocols where supported, such as HTTPS or encrypted VPN protocols.

Because users may import their own profiles or use third-party servers, VDTVPN cannot control the security practices of those third-party services.

Data Retention and Deletion

VDTVPN stores most app data locally on the user’s device, including app settings, imported profiles, selected profile, and subscription URLs.

Users can delete local data by:

  • Removing profiles or subscription data inside the app where available.
  • Clearing app data in Android settings.
  • Uninstalling the app.

If a user uses a third-party subscription provider or account, account deletion and server-side data deletion must be requested from that provider. For VDTVPN support requests, users may contact us at the email below.

Children

VDTVPN is not designed for children. The app is intended for users who understand and manage their own network connection profiles. Users must comply with applicable laws and third-party service terms.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we update it, we will change the effective date above. Continued use of VDTVPN after an update means the user accepts the updated Privacy Policy.
